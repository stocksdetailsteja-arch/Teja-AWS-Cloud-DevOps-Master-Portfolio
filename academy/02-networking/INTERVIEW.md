# 🌐 Cloud Networking: Interview Questions

1. What problem does this technology solve?
2. Explain its main architecture and control flow.
3. Where is state stored and how is it protected?
4. What are the common failure modes?
5. Which commands and signals establish health?
6. How are security boundaries enforced?
7. How does it scale and what becomes a bottleneck?
8. What is the rollback strategy?
9. What is the disaster-recovery approach?
10. What costs remain when idle?
11. Describe one failure you reproduced and resolved.
12. Which design alternative did you reject and why?
